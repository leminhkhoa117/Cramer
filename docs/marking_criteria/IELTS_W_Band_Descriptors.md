## Writing Task 1: Band Descriptors
*(Scoring criteria for Academic and General Training tests)*

| Band | Task Achievement | Coherence & Cohesion | Lexical Resource | Grammatical Range & Accuracy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **9** | **All the requirements of the task are fully and appropriately satisfied.**<br><br>There may be extremely rare lapses in content. | **The message can be followed effortlessly.**<br><br>Cohesion is used in such a way that it very rarely attracts attention.<br><br>Any lapses in coherence or cohesion are minimal.<br><br>Paragraphing is skilfully managed. | **Full flexibility and precise use are evident within the scope of the task.**<br><br>A wide range of vocabulary is used accurately and appropriately with very natural and sophisticated control of lexical features.<br><br>Minor errors in spelling and word formation are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication. | **A wide range of structures within the scope of the task is used with full flexibility and control.**<br><br>Punctuation and grammar are used appropriately throughout.<br><br>Minor errors are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication. |
| **8** | **The response covers all the requirements of the task appropriately, relevantly and sufficiently.**<br><br>*(Academic)* Key features are skilfully selected, and clearly presented, highlighted and illustrated.<br><br>*(General Training)* All bullet points are clearly presented, and appropriately illustrated or extended.<br><br>There may be occasional omissions or lapses in content. | **The message can be followed with ease.**<br><br>Information and ideas are logically sequenced, and cohesion is well managed.<br><br>Occasional lapses in coherence or cohesion may occur.<br><br>Paragraphing is used sufficiently and appropriately. | **A wide resource is fluently and flexibly used to convey precise meanings within the scope of the task.**<br><br>There is skilful use of uncommon and/or idiomatic items when appropriate, despite occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation.<br><br>Occasional errors in spelling and/or word formation may occur, but have minimal impact on communication. | **A wide range of structures within the scope of the task is flexibly and accurately used.**<br><br>The majority of sentences are error-free, and punctuation is well managed.<br><br>Occasional, non-systematic errors and inappropriacies occur, but have minimal impact on communication. |
| **7** | **The response covers the requirements of the task.**<br><br>The content is relevant and accurate - there may be a few omissions or lapses.<br><br>The format is appropriate.<br><br>*(Academic)* Key features which are selected are covered and clearly highlighted but could be more fully or more appropriately illustrated or extended.<br><br>*(Academic)* It presents a clear overview, the data are appropriately categorised, and main trends or differences are identified.<br><br>*(General Training)* All bullet points are covered and clearly highlighted but could be more fully or more appropriately illustrated or extended. It presents a clear purpose. The tone is consistent and appropriate to the task. Any lapses are minimal. | **Information and ideas are logically organised and there is a clear progression throughout the response.** A few lapses may occur.<br><br>A range of cohesive devices including reference and substitution is used flexibly but with some inaccuracies or some over/under use. | **The resource is sufficient to allow some flexibility and precision.**<br><br>There is some ability to use less common and/or idiomatic items.<br><br>An awareness of style and collocation is evident, though inappropriacies occur.<br><br>There are only a few errors in spelling and/or word formation, and they do not detract from overall clarity. | **A variety of complex structures is used with some flexibility and accuracy.**<br><br>Grammar and punctuation are generally well controlled, and error-free sentences are frequent.<br><br>A few errors in grammar may persist, but these do not impede communication. |
| **6** | **The response focuses on the requirements of the task and an appropriate format is used.**<br><br>*(Academic)* Key features which are selected are covered and adequately highlighted. A relevant overview is attempted. Information is appropriately selected and supported using figures/data.<br><br>*(General Training)* All bullet points are covered and adequately highlighted. The purpose is generally clear. There may be minor inconsistencies in tone.<br><br>Some irrelevant, inappropriate or inaccurate information may occur in areas of detail or when illustrating or extending the main points.<br><br>Some details may be missing (or excessive) and further extension or illustration may be needed. | **Information and ideas are generally arranged coherently and there is a clear overall progression.**<br><br>Cohesive devices are used to some good effect but cohesion within and/or between sentences may be faulty or mechanical due to misuse, overuse or omission.<br><br>The use of reference and substitution may lack flexibility or clarity and result in some repetition or error. | **The resource is generally adequate and appropriate for the task.**<br><br>The meaning is generally clear in spite of a rather restricted range or a lack of precision in word choice.<br><br>If the writer is a risk-taker, there will be a wider range of vocabulary used but higher degrees of inaccuracy or inappropriacy.<br><br>There are some errors in spelling and/or word formation, but these do not impede communication. | **A mix of simple and complex sentence forms is used but flexibility is limited.**<br><br>Examples of more complex structures are not marked by the same level of accuracy as in simple structures.<br><br>Errors in grammar and punctuation occur, but rarely impede communication. |
| **5** | **The response generally addresses the requirements of the task. The format may be inappropriate in places.**<br><br>*(Academic)* Key features which are selected are not adequately covered. The recounting of detail is mainly mechanical. There may be no data to support the description.<br><br>*(General Training)* All bullet points are presented but one or more may not be adequately covered. The purpose may be unclear at times. The tone may be variable and sometimes inappropriate.<br><br>There may be a tendency to focus on details (without referring to the bigger picture).<br><br>The inclusion of irrelevant, inappropriate or inaccurate material in key areas detracts from the task achievement.<br><br>There is limited detail when extending and illustrating the main points. | **Organisation is evident but is not wholly logical and there may be a lack of overall progression.** Nevertheless, there is a sense of underlying coherence to the response.<br><br>The relationship of ideas can be followed but the sentences are not fluently linked to each other.<br><br>There may be limited/overuse of cohesive devices with some inaccuracy.<br><br>The writing may be repetitive due to inadequate and/or inaccurate use of reference and substitution. | **The resource is limited but minimally adequate for the task.**<br><br>Simple vocabulary may be used accurately but the range does not permit much variation in expression.<br><br>There may be frequent lapses in the appropriacy of word choice, and a lack of flexibility is apparent in frequent simplifications and/or repetitions.<br><br>Errors in spelling and/or word formation may be noticeable and may cause some difficulty for the reader. | **The range of structures is limited and rather repetitive.**<br><br>Although complex sentences are attempted, they tend to be faulty, and the greatest accuracy is achieved on simple sentences.<br><br>Grammatical errors may be frequent and cause some difficulty for the reader.<br><br>Punctuation may be faulty. |
| **4** | **The response is an attempt to address the task.**<br><br>*(Academic)* Few key features have been selected.<br><br>*(General Training)* Not all bullet points are presented.<br><br>*(General Training)* The purpose of the letter is not clearly explained and may be confused. The tone may be inappropriate.<br><br>The format may be inappropriate.<br><br>Key features/bullet points which are presented may be irrelevant, repetitive, inaccurate or inappropriate. | **Information and ideas are evident but not arranged coherently, and there is no clear progression within the response.**<br><br>Relationships between ideas can be unclear and/or inadequately marked. There is some use of basic cohesive devices, which may be inaccurate or repetitive.<br><br>There is inaccurate use or a lack of substitution or referencing. | **The resource is limited and inadequate for or unrelated to the task.**<br><br>Vocabulary is basic and may be used repetitively.<br><br>There may be inappropriate use of lexical chunks (e.g. memorised phrases, formulaic language and/or language from the input material).<br><br>Inappropriate word choice and/or errors in word formation and/or in spelling may impede meaning. | **A very limited range of structures is used.**<br><br>Subordinate clauses are rare and simple sentences predominate.<br><br>Some structures are produced accurately but grammatical errors are frequent and may impede meaning.<br><br>Punctuation is often faulty or inadequate. |
| **3** | **The response does not address the requirements of the task (possibly because of misunderstanding of the data/diagram/situation).**<br><br>Key features/bullet points which are presented may be largely irrelevant.<br><br>Limited information is presented, and this may be used repetitively.<br><br>The content barely relates to the task. | **There is no apparent logical organisation.** Ideas are discernible but difficult to relate to each other.<br><br>Minimal use of sequencers or cohesive devices. Those used do not necessarily indicate a logical relationship between ideas.<br><br>There is difficulty in identifying referencing.<br><br>There is little evidence of control of organisational features. | **The resource is inadequate (which may be due to the response being significantly underlength).**<br><br>Possible over-dependence on input material or memorised language.<br><br>Control of word choice and/or spelling is very limited, and errors predominate. These errors may severely impede meaning.<br><br>The resource is extremely limited with few recognisable strings, apart from memorised phrases. | **Sentence forms are attempted, but errors in grammar and punctuation predominate (except in memorised phrases or those taken from the input material).** This prevents most meaning from coming through.<br><br>Length may be insufficient to provide evidence of control of sentence forms.<br><br>There is little or no evidence of sentence forms (except in memorised phrases). |
| **2** | **The content is barely related to the task.**<br><br>There is little relevant message, or the entire response may be off-topic.<br><br>*(Note: Responses of 20 words or fewer are rated at Band 1.)* | **There is little relevant message, or the entire response may be off-topic.** | **The resource is extremely limited with few recognisable strings, apart from memorised phrases.**<br><br>There is no apparent control of word formation and/or spelling. | **There is little or no evidence of sentence forms (except in memorised phrases).** |
| **1** | **The content is wholly unrelated to the task.**<br><br>Any copied rubric must be discounted.<br><br>*(Note: Responses of 20 words or fewer are rated at Band 1.)* | **The writing fails to communicate any message and appears to be by a virtual non-writer.** | **No resource is apparent, except for a few isolated words.** | **No rateable language is evident.** |
| **0** | **Should only be used where a candidate did not attend or attempt the question in any way, used a language other than English throughout, or where there is proof that a candidate's answer has been totally memorised.** | | | |

## Writing Task 2: Band Descriptors
*(Scoring criteria for Academic and General Training tests)*

| Band | Task Response | Coherence & Cohesion | Lexical Resource | Grammatical Range & Accuracy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **9** | **The prompt is appropriately addressed and explored in depth.**<br><br>A clear and fully developed position is presented which directly answers the question/s.<br><br>Ideas are relevant, fully extended and well supported.<br><br>Any lapses in content or support are extremely rare. | **The message can be followed effortlessly.**<br><br>Cohesion is used in such a way that it very rarely attracts attention.<br><br>Any lapses in coherence or cohesion are minimal.<br><br>Paragraphing is skilfully managed. | **Full flexibility and precise use are widely evident.**<br><br>A wide range of vocabulary is used accurately and appropriately with very natural and sophisticated control of lexical features.<br><br>Minor errors in spelling and word formation are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication. | **A wide range of structures is used with full flexibility and control.**<br><br>Punctuation and grammar are used appropriately throughout.<br><br>Minor errors are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication. |
| **8** | **The prompt is appropriately and sufficiently addressed.**<br><br>A clear and well-developed position is presented in response to the question/s.<br><br>Ideas are relevant, well extended and supported.<br><br>There may be occasional omissions or lapses in content. | **The message can be followed with ease.**<br><br>Information and ideas are logically sequenced, and cohesion is well managed.<br><br>Occasional lapses in coherence and cohesion may occur.<br><br>Paragraphing is used sufficiently and appropriately. | **A wide resource is fluently and flexibly used to convey precise meanings.**<br><br>There is skilful use of uncommon and/or idiomatic items when appropriate, despite occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation.<br><br>Occasional errors in spelling and/or word formation may occur, but have minimal impact on communication. | **A wide range of structures is flexibly and accurately used.**<br><br>The majority of sentences are error-free, and punctuation is well managed.<br><br>Occasional, non-systematic errors and inappropriacies occur, but have minimal impact on communication. |
| **7** | **The main parts of the prompt are appropriately addressed.**<br><br>A clear and developed position is presented.<br><br>Main ideas are extended and supported but there may be a tendency to over-generalise or there may be a lack of focus and precision in supporting ideas/material. | **Information and ideas are logically organised, and there is a clear progression throughout the response.** (A few lapses may occur, but these are minor.)<br><br>A range of cohesive devices including reference and substitution is used flexibly but with some inaccuracies or some over/under use.<br><br>Paragraphing is generally used effectively to support overall coherence, and the sequencing of ideas within a paragraph is generally logical. | **The resource is sufficient to allow some flexibility and precision.**<br><br>There is some ability to use less common and/or idiomatic items.<br><br>An awareness of style and collocation is evident, though inappropriacies occur.<br><br>There are only a few errors in spelling and/or word formation and they do not detract from overall clarity. | **A variety of complex structures is used with some flexibility and accuracy.**<br><br>Grammar and punctuation are generally well controlled, and error-free sentences are frequent.<br><br>A few errors in grammar may persist, but these do not impede communication. |
| **6** | **The main parts of the prompt are addressed (though some may be more fully covered than others). An appropriate format is used.**<br><br>A position is presented that is directly relevant to the prompt, although the conclusions drawn may be unclear, unjustified or repetitive.<br><br>Main ideas are relevant, but some may be insufficiently developed or may lack clarity, while some supporting arguments and evidence may be less relevant or inadequate. | **Information and ideas are generally arranged coherently and there is a clear overall progression.**<br><br>Cohesive devices are used to some good effect but cohesion within and/or between sentences may be faulty or mechanical due to misuse, overuse or omission.<br><br>The use of reference and substitution may lack flexibility or clarity and result in some repetition or error.<br><br>Paragraphing may not always be logical and/or the central topic may not always be clear. | **The resource is generally adequate and appropriate for the task.**<br><br>The meaning is generally clear in spite of a rather restricted range or a lack of precision in word choice.<br><br>If the writer is a risk-taker, there will be a wider range of vocabulary used but higher degrees of inaccuracy or inappropriacy.<br><br>There are some errors in spelling and/or word formation, but these do not impede communication. | **A mix of simple and complex sentence forms is used but flexibility is limited.**<br><br>Examples of more complex structures are not marked by the same level of accuracy as in simple structures.<br><br>Errors in grammar and punctuation occur, but rarely impede communication. |
| **5** | **The main parts of the prompt are incompletely addressed. The format may be inappropriate in places.**<br><br>The writer expresses a position, but the development is not always clear.<br><br>Some main ideas are put forward, but they are limited and are not sufficiently developed and/or there may be irrelevant detail.<br><br>There may be some repetition. | **Organisation is evident but is not wholly logical and there may be a lack of overall progression.** Nevertheless, there is a sense of underlying coherence to the response.<br><br>The relationship of ideas can be followed but the sentences are not fluently linked to each other.<br><br>There may be limited/overuse of cohesive devices with some inaccuracy.<br><br>The writing may be repetitive due to inadequate and/or inaccurate use of reference and substitution.<br><br>Paragraphing may be inadequate or missing. | **The resource is limited but minimally adequate for the task.**<br><br>Simple vocabulary may be used accurately but the range does not permit much variation in expression.<br><br>There may be frequent lapses in the appropriacy of word choice and a lack of flexibility is apparent in frequent simplifications and/or repetitions.<br><br>Errors in spelling and/or word formation may be noticeable and may cause some difficulty for the reader. | **The range of structures is limited and rather repetitive.**<br><br>Although complex sentences are attempted, they tend to be faulty, and the greatest accuracy is achieved on simple sentences.<br><br>Grammatical errors may be frequent and cause some difficulty for the reader.<br><br>Punctuation may be faulty. |
| **4** | **The prompt is tackled in a minimal way, or the answer is tangential, possibly due to some misunderstanding of the prompt. The format may be inappropriate.**<br><br>A position is discernible, but the reader has to read carefully to find it.<br><br>Main ideas are difficult to identify and such ideas that are identifiable may lack relevance, clarity and/or support.<br><br>Large parts of the response may be repetitive. | **Information and ideas are evident but not arranged coherently and there is no clear progression within the response.**<br><br>Relationships between ideas can be unclear and/or inadequately marked. There is some use of basic cohesive devices, which may be inaccurate or repetitive.<br><br>There is inaccurate use or a lack of substitution or referencing.<br><br>There may be no paragraphing and/or no clear main topic within paragraphs. | **The resource is limited and inadequate for or unrelated to the task. Vocabulary is basic and may be used repetitively.**<br><br>There may be inappropriate use of lexical chunks (e.g. memorised phrases, formulaic language and/or language from the input material).<br><br>Inappropriate word choice and/or errors in word formation and/or in spelling may impede meaning. | **A very limited range of structures is used.**<br><br>Subordinate clauses are rare and simple sentences predominate.<br><br>Some structures are produced accurately but grammatical errors are frequent and may impede meaning.<br><br>Punctuation is often faulty or inadequate. |
| **3** | **No part of the prompt is adequately addressed, or the prompt has been misunderstood.**<br><br>No relevant position can be identified, and/or there is little direct response to the question/s.<br><br>There are few ideas, and these may be irrelevant or insufficiently developed.<br><br>The content is barely related to the prompt.<br><br>No position can be identified.<br><br>There may be glimpses of one or two ideas without development. | **There is no apparent logical organisation. Ideas are discernible but difficult to relate to each other.**<br><br>There is minimal use of sequencers or cohesive devices. Those used do not necessarily indicate a logical relationship between ideas.<br><br>There is difficulty in identifying referencing.<br><br>Any attempts at paragraphing are unhelpful.<br><br>There is little relevant message, or the entire response may be off-topic.<br><br>There is little evidence of control of organisational features. | **The resource is inadequate (which may be due to the response being significantly underlength).**<br><br>Possible over-dependence on input material or memorised language.<br><br>Control of word choice and/or spelling is very limited, and errors predominate. These errors may severely impede meaning.<br><br>The resource is extremely limited with few recognisable strings, apart from memorised phrases. | **Sentence forms are attempted, but errors in grammar and punctuation predominate (except in memorised phrases or those taken from the input material).** This prevents most meaning from coming through.<br><br>Length may be insufficient to provide evidence of control of sentence forms.<br><br>There is little or no evidence of sentence forms (except in memorised phrases). |
| **2** | **The content is barely related to the prompt.**<br><br>No position can be identified. | **There is no apparent logical organisation.** | **The resource is extremely limited with few recognisable strings, apart from memorised phrases.**<br><br>There is no apparent control of word formation and/or spelling. | **There is little or no evidence of sentence forms (except in memorised phrases).** |
| **1** | **The content is wholly unrelated to the prompt.**<br><br>Any copied rubric must be discounted.<br><br>*(Note: Responses of 20 words or fewer are rated at Band 1.)* | **The writing fails to communicate any message and appears to be by a virtual non-writer.** | **No resource is apparent, except for a few isolated words.** | **No rateable language is evident.** |
| **0** | **Should only be used where a candidate did not attend or attempt the question in any way, used a language other than English throughout, or where there is proof that a candidate's answer has been totally memorised.** | | | |
