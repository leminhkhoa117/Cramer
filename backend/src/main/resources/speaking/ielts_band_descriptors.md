# IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors (Public Version)

> Source: Official IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors (public domain).
> Used as rubric grounding for the AI examiner prompt.

## Fluency & Coherence

This criterion assesses how fluently the candidate speaks and how well ideas are connected.

- **Band 9**: Speaks fluently with only rare repetition or self-correction; any hesitation is content-related rather than to find words or grammar. Speaks coherently with fully appropriate cohesive features. Develops topics fully and appropriately. Uses a full range of cohesive features and discourse markers spontaneously and precisely. Speech is effortless and natural throughout.
- **Band 8**: Speaks fluently with only occasional repetition or self-correction; hesitation is usually content-related and only rarely to search for language. Develops topics coherently and appropriately. Uses a range of cohesive features appropriately and flexibly. May occasionally lose coherence when dealing with highly abstract or unfamiliar topics, but quickly recovers.
- **Band 7**: Speaks at length without noticeable effort or loss of coherence. May demonstrate language-related hesitation at times, or some repetition and/or self-correction. Uses a range of connectives and discourse markers with some flexibility. Can generally maintain the flow of speech while self-correcting or rephrasing. Topic development is adequate and mostly relevant to the task.
- **Band 6**: Is willing to speak at length, though may lose coherence at times due to occasional repetition, self-correction or hesitation. Uses a range of connectives and discourse markers but not always appropriately. Topic development may be somewhat limited or uneven, with some irrelevant details. Can usually be understood, but listener effort may increase at times.
- **Band 5**: Usually maintains flow of speech but uses repetition, self-correction and/or slow speech to keep going. May over-use certain connectives and discourse markers (e.g., "and", "but", "also"). Produces simple speech fluently, but more complex communication causes fluency problems. Topic development is limited, often staying on safe, familiar ground.
- **Band 4**: Cannot respond without noticeable pauses and may speak slowly, with frequent repetition and self-correction. Links basic sentences but with repetitious use of simple connectives and some breakdowns in coherence. Responses are limited to short, simple utterances. Difficulty developing topics beyond basic statements.
- **Band 3**: Speaks with long pauses. Has limited ability to link simple sentences. Gives only simple responses and is frequently unable to convey basic message. Communication is heavily dependent on the examiner's prompts and scaffolding.
- **Band 2**: Speaks with long pauses. Has very limited ability to link ideas. Produces mostly isolated words or memorized phrases. Unable to construct even short connected speech.
- **Band 1**: Essentially unable to speak. May produce occasional isolated words. No meaningful communication beyond individual word attempts.
- **Band 0**: Does not attend / no assessable speech.

## Lexical Resource

This criterion assesses the range, accuracy, and appropriateness of vocabulary used.

- **Band 9**: Uses vocabulary with full flexibility and precision in all topics. Uses idiomatic language naturally and accurately. Demonstrates sophisticated awareness of connotation, register, and collocation. Paraphrases effortlessly and elegantly. Word choice is consistently precise and effective.
- **Band 8**: Uses a wide vocabulary resource readily and flexibly to convey precise meaning. Uses less common and idiomatic vocabulary skilfully, with occasional inaccuracies. Uses paraphrase effectively as required. Style and register are generally appropriate. Minor lexical gaps are handled smoothly with circumlocution.
- **Band 7**: Uses vocabulary resource flexibly to discuss a variety of topics. Uses some less common and idiomatic vocabulary and shows some awareness of style and collocation, with some inappropriate choices. Uses paraphrase effectively to fill lexical gaps. May occasionally rely on familiar vocabulary for complex ideas.
- **Band 6**: Has a wide enough vocabulary to discuss topics at length and make meaning clear in spite of inappropriacies. Generally paraphrases successfully. Vocabulary may be adequate for most everyday topics, but less precise for abstract or specialized subjects. Errors in word choice occur but rarely cause misunderstanding.
- **Band 5**: Manages to talk about familiar and unfamiliar topics but uses vocabulary with limited flexibility. Attempts to use paraphrase but with mixed success. Word choice is often imprecise or repetitive. May rely heavily on a limited set of high-frequency words.
- **Band 4**: Is able to talk about familiar topics but can only convey basic meaning on unfamiliar topics and makes frequent errors in word choice. Rarely attempts paraphrase. Vocabulary is limited to simple, concrete words. Errors may obscure meaning at times.
- **Band 3**: Uses simple vocabulary to convey personal information. Has insufficient vocabulary for less familiar topics. Frequently searches for words or substitutes with L1. Meaning is often unclear due to lexical gaps.
- **Band 2**: Only produces isolated words or memorised phrases. No meaningful lexical range.
- **Band 1**: Essentially unable to produce any words beyond isolated words.
- **Band 0**: Does not attend / no assessable speech.

## Grammatical Range & Accuracy

This criterion assesses the range and accuracy of grammatical structures used.

- **Band 9**: Uses a full range of structures naturally and appropriately. Produces consistently accurate structures apart from 'slips' characteristic of native speaker speech. Demonstrates mastery of complex sentence patterns, including embedded clauses, conditionals, passive constructions, and inversion. Errors are virtually non-existent.
- **Band 8**: Uses a wide range of structures flexibly. Produces a majority of error-free sentences with only very occasional inappropriacies or basic/non-systematic errors. Complex structures are used confidently, though minor errors may persist in the most ambitious constructions.
- **Band 7**: Uses a range of complex structures with some flexibility. Frequently produces error-free sentences, though some grammatical mistakes persist. Errors are generally non-systematic and do not cause misunderstanding. May avoid the most demanding structures under pressure.
- **Band 6**: Uses a mix of simple and complex structures, but with limited flexibility. May make frequent mistakes with complex structures, though these rarely cause comprehension problems. Simple structures are generally accurate. Complex sentences are attempted but often contain errors.
- **Band 5**: Produces basic sentence forms with reasonable accuracy. Uses a limited range of more complex structures, but these usually contain errors and may cause some comprehension problems. Grammatical control is sufficient in familiar contexts but breaks down in less predictable situations.
- **Band 4**: Produces basic sentence forms and some correct simple sentences but subordinate structures are rare. Errors are frequent and may lead to misunderstanding. Sentence boundaries may be unclear. L1 interference is noticeable in word order and structure.
- **Band 3**: Attempts basic sentence forms but with limited success, or relies on apparently memorised utterances. Makes numerous errors except in memorised expressions. Communication is severely limited by grammatical inaccuracy.
- **Band 2**: Cannot produce basic sentence forms. Utterances are limited to two-to-three-word phrases with no grammatical control.
- **Band 1**: Essentially unable to produce any sentence forms.
- **Band 0**: Does not attend / no assessable speech.

## Pronunciation

This criterion assesses the clarity, intelligibility, and phonological control of the candidate's speech.

- **Band 9**: Uses a full range of pronunciation features with precision and subtlety. Sustains flexible use of features throughout. Is effortless to understand. Demonstrates mastery of stress, intonation, rhythm, connected speech, and individual sounds. L1 accent is undetectable or has no effect whatsoever on intelligibility.
- **Band 8**: Uses a wide range of pronunciation features. Sustains flexible use of features, with only occasional lapses. Is easy to understand throughout. L1 accent has minimal effect on intelligibility. Word and sentence stress are generally well controlled. Intonation patterns are varied and appropriate.
- **Band 7**: Shows all the positive features of Band 6 and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band 8. Pronunciation is generally clear with occasional mispronunciation of individual words or sounds. Stress and intonation are generally appropriate but may show occasional L1 interference.
- **Band 6**: Uses a range of pronunciation features with mixed control. Shows some effective use of features but this is not sustained. Can generally be understood throughout, though mispronunciation of individual words or sounds occasionally reduces clarity. L1 accent may be noticeable but does not prevent understanding.
- **Band 5**: Shows all the positive features of Band 4 and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band 6. Pronunciation is usually intelligible but L1 accent is prominent and causes occasional difficulty for the listener. Stress and intonation patterns show strong L1 influence.
- **Band 4**: Uses a limited range of pronunciation features. Attempts to control features but lapses are frequent. Mispronunciations are frequent and cause some difficulty for the listener. L1 phonological features dominate. Connected speech is poorly controlled.
- **Band 3**: Shows all the positive features of Band 2 and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band 4. Speech is often difficult to understand due to frequent mispronunciations. Very limited control of phonological features.
- **Band 2**: Speech is often unintelligible. L1 accent severely impedes communication. Individual words may be recognizable but connected speech is essentially absent.
- **Band 1**: Speech is virtually unintelligible. At best, isolated words may be recognizable.
- **Band 0**: Does not attend / no assessable speech.
